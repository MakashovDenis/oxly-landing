FAQ

What is NIS2?

The NIS2 Directive is an EU-wide cybersecurity framework. Many companies in Germany must meet certain requirements. This kit helps you understand and implement the most important steps.

Who is the kit suitable for?

For small and medium-sized companies in Germany that want to prepare for NIS2 and the AI Act. The kit offers a structured starting point; individual legal or compliance advice may still be required.

How do I get access?

After purchase you receive an email with the download link. Access is available immediately.

Is this legal advice?

No. The kit is a practical aid for orientation and preparation. It does not replace individual legal or compliance advice.
