Information on the right of withdrawal when purchasing digital content via Lemon Squeezy.

1. Contracting party and scope

The purchase of the “NIS2 & AI Act Readiness Kit” (digital content not supplied on a tangible medium) is processed through Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. The buyer’s contracting party for the purchase contract is therefore Lemon Squeezy. The provider and licensor of the content is Oxly GmbH. The Buyer Terms of Lemon Squeezy apply.

The statutory right of withdrawal under sections 355 and 356 of the German Civil Code (BGB) is available to consumers within the meaning of section 13 BGB. There is no statutory right of withdrawal vis-à-vis entrepreneurs within the meaning of section 14 BGB.

The notice below follows the statutory model in Annex 1 to Article 246a section 1(2) sentence 2 of the Introductory Act to the German Civil Code (EGBGB) (gesetze-im-internet.de) and is tailored to the sale of digital content. The model withdrawal form corresponds to Annex 2 to Article 246a section 1(2) sentence 1 no. 1 EGBGB (gesetze-im-internet.de).

2. Contact details for withdrawal

Withdrawal should in the first instance be addressed to the contracting party (Lemon Squeezy). In the “Contact” section of Lemon Squeezy’s Buyer Terms (as last reviewed in November 2026, https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/buyer-terms), the following address is stated for purchases in the United States:

On other official Lemon Squeezy pages (including https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing), the contact address hello@lemonsqueezy.com is given; we state it below as a precaution, without inventing any further means of contact.

You may also send a withdrawal declaration additionally to Oxly GmbH, Schönenberger Str. 49, 53783 Eitorf, Germany, email: io@oxly.io. We will forward such a declaration to Lemon Squeezy. To meet the deadline, we recommend addressing the withdrawal to Lemon Squeezy directly wherever possible and informing Oxly GmbH only in addition.

3. Early expiry of the right of withdrawal for digital content

The subject of the contract is the supply of digital content that is not supplied on a tangible medium. Under section 356(6) BGB, the right of withdrawal in a contract that obliges the consumer to pay a price expires only if all of the following conditions are met:

  • the trader has begun performance of the contract,
  • the consumer has expressly consented to the trader beginning performance of the contract before the withdrawal period has expired,
  • the consumer has confirmed their knowledge that, by giving this consent, the right of withdrawal expires once performance of the contract begins, and
  • the trader has provided the consumer with a confirmation pursuant to section 312f BGB.

These conditions must actually be fulfilled in the Lemon Squeezy checkout. A mere clause in general terms and conditions is regularly not sufficient. If the statutory conditions are not met, the right of withdrawal remains unaffected.

Source of the statutory conditions: section 356(6) BGB and section 312f BGB.
