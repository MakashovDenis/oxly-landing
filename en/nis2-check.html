NIS2 quick check: Does NIS2 apply to you?

Seven short questions for a preliminary classification under the German BSI Act (BSIG) as amended by the NIS2 Implementation Act (NIS2-Umsetzungsgesetz). The check runs entirely in your browser. No answers are stored or sent to us or to third parties.

This check is for orientation only. It is not legal advice, not a binding statement by the competent authority, and not a substitute for an individual assessment (for example against the official wording, affiliated companies or fine distinctions in the annexes). The underlying rules were last reviewed on 11 November 2026.

1. Does one of these special cases apply to your organisation?

Some entities are in scope regardless of size, or under special size thresholds.

2. In which area is your organisation mainly active?

The lists summarise the official annexes; the wording of the relevant type of entity in Annex 1 or 2 of the BSIG is decisive.

3. How many employees does your organisation have?

As a rule, the thresholds of Recommendation 2003/361/EC apply, subject to section 28(4) BSIG (without Article 3(4) of the Annex to the Recommendation). Affiliated companies may count; if in doubt, select unsure.

4. What is the annual turnover?
5. What is the annual balance-sheet total?
6. Does your organisation offer goods or services?

The sectoral thresholds in section 28(1) no. 4 and section 28(2) no. 3 BSIG apply where goods or services are offered. Special cases (for example KRITIS) may apply independently of this.

7. Do you use AI systems in your organisation (including common office or specialist applications with AI features)?

This question does not change the NIS2 result. It is used for the note on AI literacy under Article 4 of the AI Act.
