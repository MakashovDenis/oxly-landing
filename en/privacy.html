2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is provided as a static site via GitHub Pages. The hosting provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (part of the Microsoft group).

When the pages are accessed, server log data is typically processed, in particular IP address, date and time of access, the file requested, the volume of data transferred, a message about successful retrieval, browser type and version, and the operating system. GitHub processes this data in order to deliver the website, to keep the infrastructure secure and to investigate disruptions. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functioning operation of the website).

Processing may take place in the United States. GitHub or Microsoft participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The retention period for log data is determined by GitHub’s own rules and processes; we do not apply a separate retention period of our own.

4. No cookies and no reach analysis on this website

This website itself does not set cookies and does not embed analytics or advertising tools (no Google Analytics, no pixel, no tag manager). Merely opening the pages does not cause us to store tracking cookies.

If you click “Buy now” or submit the contact or waitlist form, you leave the purely static part of this website or use an external service. Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their notices then apply. The NIS2 quick check on this website does not transmit any inputs (see section 8).

The “NIS2 & AI Act Readiness Kit” is purchased through Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record (contracting party and payment processor, including the handling of VAT). The “Buy now” buttons lead to the Lemon Squeezy checkout page at oxly.lemonsqueezy.com.

The Lemon Squeezy checkout page is a separate website. Processing takes place in order to perform the purchase contract or pre-contractual measures, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, and to fulfil tax and commercial-law obligations, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Details of cookies, providers used, retention periods and any transfers to third countries are set out in Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy: https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

6. Contact form and email

You can send us your name, email address and a message via the contact form. Use of the form is voluntary; required fields are marked. You can also write to us directly at io@oxly.io.

The form is delivered to our address io@oxly.io via the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co, provider information: Devro LABS, contact according to the provider: support@formsubmit.co). FormSubmit processes the details you enter in order to forward the message to us by email. We do not have a data-processing agreement concluded by us, nor a server location published by the provider. It is therefore possible that the data is processed in the United States or another third country. Where such a transfer is necessary in order to handle the enquiry you submitted via the form (performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures at your request), we rely, as a precaution, on Article 49(1)(b) GDPR for any transfer to a third country. Article 49(1) GDPR is an exception and is to be interpreted narrowly. We are not aware of an adequacy decision or of standard contractual clauses concluded by us with the provider. The provider’s privacy policy: https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf.

A hidden honeypot field is included to help prevent spam. We have not enabled FormSubmit’s optional captcha service (Google reCAPTCHA), so that this website does not load reCAPTCHA or the Google cookies associated with it.

Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the message serves the initiation or performance of a contract; otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in handling incoming enquiries). The consent given via the required checkbox acknowledging this policy and agreeing to processing for the purpose of the enquiry additionally supports processing under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future, for example by email to io@oxly.io.

We store enquiries in our email inbox for as long as they are needed to handle them, and then delete them unless statutory retention obligations apply (for example if the enquiry leads to a contractual relationship).

8. NIS2 quick check

The “NIS2 quick check” on this website runs entirely in your browser. The answers you select are not transmitted to us, to FormSubmit, to Lemon Squeezy or to any other party, and are not stored by us. There is no server-side evaluation. If you use your browser’s print or PDF function, the process remains with you.
